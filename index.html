§ 01 — About

Engineer first, but I care about how things feel.

I'm a full-stack engineer with three years across QA, automation, and shipping production systems. I started in quality assurance, which is probably why I'm uncomfortable shipping anything that breaks on the second click.

These days I write Python, TypeScript, and (increasingly) Rust — building APIs, deploying to Kubernetes, and wiring up LLM tooling that does real work instead of demos.

Outside of work I build for the web — a font-recognition browser extension that runs entirely offline, an AI orchestration platform that reads tickets and writes pull requests, a clothing storefront. I like problems that sit at the seam between systems.
